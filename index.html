Dr. Ankita Vilasrao Bhagat is a highly skilled dental surgeon with a BDS degree from Maharashtra University of Health Sciences (MUHS). With years of hands-on experience in general and cosmetic dentistry, she brings a compassionate, patient-first approach to every treatment.
